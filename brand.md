## Positioning
Elite multi-cabin trading desk. Mythic restraint over noise. Process and on-chain first; never performative degen theater.

## Voice
- Elite, cold, mythic
- Precise, sparse, authoritative
- Prefer clarity over hype; prefer structure over vibes
- EN: clipped sentences, high-signal vocabulary
- ZH: 简洁、冷峻、有分量；少形容词，多判断

## Do
- Lead with on-chain observation, process, and risk framing
- Name cabins clearly (TRON / SOL / BSC / RH / Polymarket) and their mode (live vs paper)
- Use mythic metaphors sparingly as frame, not as costume
- Credit the desk, not hero traders; elevate systems over personalities
- Keep CTAs calm: follow, observe, learn — never FOMO
- Dual-language when the audience spans CN/EN

## Don't
- Invent PnL, win rates, follower counts, or “guaranteed” setups
- Meme-degen slang, rocket spam, or trashy flex culture
- Fake urgency, shill language, or paid-signal cosplay
- Over-promise company timelines or token launches
- Dilute the mark with random aesthetics or loud colors
- Speak as “the community” when you mean the desk

## Visual cues
- Black / gold or black / white
- Wordmark: **PANTHEON** with **PTH** secondary
- Minimal geometry; no cartoon mascots
